What to do
We put together a little guide of our favorite things to eat and drink, places to explore & more.
Museum
César Manrique Foundation
The César Manrique House Museum is located in a handsome palm grove in the picturesque village of Haria, which still conserves many of Lanzarote’s traditions. In Haría, the artist found the quietude and harmony with nature that he had always pursued.
Brewery
Cervecera Malpeis
Cervecera Malpeis is a small brewery that produces craft beers that are brewed in a natural way, with knowledge and lots of love. The Cervecera is formed by Orlando Niz Andueza (musician and master brewer) and Tania Delgado Cabrera (agricultural engineer and oenologist).
Food
Tacanda (Haria)
Tacande was born from the illusion of offering a meal that can transport you to another country through its dishes with the traditional products of Lanzarote. It is located in the north of the island right in the centre of the Plaza Leon and Castle of Haria.

Food
La Bodega De Santiago
La Bodega de Santiago is a 200-year-old house, converted into a restaurant in 2005 in memory of Santiago Eugenio. Located in this 19th-century manor house, restored in 2003, we can offer you a pleasant peaceful setting for all your senses to enjoy our carefully prepared dishes.
Winery
Bodega STRATVS
The STRATVS Winery is located in La Geria, the vine growing area of the island of Lanzarote. It was established in 2008 with the desire to preserve and promote our tradition. It is located in a spectacular environment that is unique worldwide, with a very well thought-out and respectful architectural design, not just in keeping with the landscape around it, but also the characteristic landscape of the island of Lanzarote.
Food
El Risco (Famara)
Restaurant El Risco, is located in the town of Famara, known for its fabulous white sandy beach with kilometres long and waters full of life and colour.It is a regular place for thousands of tourists, bathing, surfing, kite surfing and other water and wind sports.
Green
Jardin de Cactus
Located in Guatiza, municipality of Teguise, El Jardín de Cactus is the last great work of César Manrique in Lanzarote, a fascinating creation that houses about 4,500 specimens of cactus of about 500 species from five continents.
Food
Costa (Famara)
Costa has a large terrace to the sea (practically bathed by the North Sea of Lanzarote) where you can taste grilled fish and seafood in all its varieties, rice, and delights of our land.
Green
Timanfaya National Park
Las Montañas del Fuego are the unusual result of the eruptions in the south of  Lanzarote in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries. A spectacle of red craters,  lava flows and lunar landscapes that reflect the burning of the earth and its  uncontrollable force.
Cave
Cueva de los Verdes
La Cueva de los Verdes is a unique formation of landscape and volcanological characteristics of extraordinary value. Located in the north of the island, and integrated into the vast volcanic landscape of the Malpaís de la Corona, it is one of the wonders hidden in the bowels of Lanzarote, endowed with a mysterious and special mythological character.
FOOD
La Cantina (Teguise)
Cantina restaurant is housed in a historic building located in the heart of Lanzarote in Teguise, the oldest town in the Canary islands and the cultural centre of the Island.
FOOD
Salmarina (3 locations)
Let all your senses be taken over in the magical and welcoming environment of our 3 Salmarina restaurants where the flavoursome dishes provide that extra special ingredient for a wonderful evening. And if you have a special event or celebration in mind, let us take care of everything! Salmarina offers you large dining halls, an excellent professional team and a taste of paradise to make your day here unforgettable.
Architecture
Mirador del Rio
Mirador del Río (Río Viewpoint) is one of the most unique architectural creations by César Manrique. It is located high up on the Risco de Famara escarpment, at an altitude of 474 metres, in the northernmost part of the island. From here you can gaze out at one of Lanzarote’s most spectacular panoramic views: the Parque Natural del Archipiélago Chinijo (Chinijo Archipelago National Park) and the Risco de Famara escarpment.
Sports
Natural Swimming Pools
On the island of Lanzarote are located some of the most impressive natural baths, called “pools”, of the entire European continent. These natural pools are the result of the work of the wind and the sea on the volcanic rocks in various places on the island; they are located on the shores of the ocean and with the beaches very close by.
Sports
Famara Surfing
Lanzarote is full of high-quality waves everywhere around the island. There are many surf spots: reef breaks, point breaks, and beach breaks to surf. The surf spot names, best tides, the best wave directions, best wind direction, and suitable surf levels for each spot.
FOOD
Mura (Teguise)
Coffee, bites and sourdough toasties.
Green
El Golfo
Amongst all of the Volcanic spectacles in Lanzarote, the green lagoon located beside the village of El Golfo is perhaps the strangest-looking and most other-worldly. The Charco or Laguna de los Clicos, to give its correct title, is also known as Lago Verde, but many simply call it ‘El Golfo’.
Beach
Playa de Papagayo
In the south of Lanzarote is one of the most popular beaches of the island, Papagayo, a cove of white sand, reduced size and great beauty. In the shape of a bay or shell (some locals prefer to call it the latter), Papagayo wins you over with its crystal clear, emerald green water which remains still all day long just like in a swimming pool.
